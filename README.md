# student-procrastination-survey
A psychology survey project exploring procrastination habits among university students.

Project Overview
A survey about procrastination among university students and the reasons students delay their academic tasks.

Objectives
To identify how often university students procrastinate.
To understand the common reasons for procrastination.
To explore how procrastination affects students' academic work.
To explore ways students can reduce procrastination.

Research Questions
1. How often do university students procrastinate?
2. What are the common reasons for procrastination among university students?
3. How does procrastination affect students' academic work?
4. what strategies can help students overcome procrastination?

Methodology
A short questionnaire will be used to collect responses from university students. Participation will be voluntary, and the survey will avoid collecting names or unnecessary personal information.

Expected Outcome
The survey aims to identify common procrastination habits and help students understand strategies for managing their academic tasks more effectively.

Project Files

Ethical Considerations
Participation will be voluntary. Responses will be treated respectfully and used only for educational purposes.

Project Status
The project is currently in the preparation stage. The questionnaire will be developed before conducting the survey.
