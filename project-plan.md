Project Plan
Procrastination Among University Students

1. Project Topic
Procrastination Among University Students.

2. Introduction
Procrastination is the habit of delaying tasks even when they need to be completed. University students may procrastinate because of distractions, stress, lack of motivation, or poor time management.

3. Objectives
To identify procrastination habits among university students.
To understand the common reasons for procrastination.
To explore its effects on academic performance.
To identify ways to reduce procrastination.

5. Methodology
A short questionnaire will be used to collect responses from university students. Participation will be voluntary, and responses will be kept anonymous.

5. Target Population
University students.

6. Expected Outcome
The project aims to understand students' procrastination habits and identify ways to improve time management and academic performance.

7. Ethical Considerations
Participation will be voluntary. No names will be required, and the collected information will be used only for educational purposes.
