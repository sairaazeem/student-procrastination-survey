Data Documentation: Procrastination Among University Students

1. Purpose
This document explains how the data collected through the procrastination survey will be recorded and organized.

2. Data Collection Method
Data will be collected through a short questionnaire completed voluntarily by university students.

3. Types of Data
The survey will collect information about:

- Students' level of study.
- How often students procrastinate.
- Reasons for delaying academic tasks.
- Effects of procrastination on studies.
- Strategies for reducing procrastination.

4. Data Recording
Each completed survey will be recorded as one response. Responses will be organized according to the survey questions.

5. Data Organization
The responses can be entered into a spreadsheet, with each row representing one participant and each column representing a survey question.

6. Privacy and Ethics
No names or unnecessary personal details will be collected. Participation will be voluntary, and the data will be used only for educational purposes.

7. Data Analysis
Responses will be counted and summarized to identify common procrastination habits, reasons, and effects among university students.

8. Project Status

The survey data will be added after responses have been collected. No results are reported at this stage.
